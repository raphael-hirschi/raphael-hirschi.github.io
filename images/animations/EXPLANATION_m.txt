Animations explanations:

#####filenames#####
file: "mxxxrotyyy....gif"
xxx: mass [Mo]
yyy: initial rotational velocity [km/s]
...: extra information about either file size or burning stages

#####TOP-LEFT#####
MvsR: Mass vs Radius

Radius: (from center until side of image) 
- left R=10^14 cm
- middle R=10^12 cm
- right R=10^10 cm

Mass: (from center to surface; % of total mass)
- white Mr< 1%
- dark blue < 10%
- middle blue < 20%
- light blue < 30%
- dark green < 40%
- middle green < 50%
- light green < 60%
- dark red < 70%
- middle red < 80%
- light red < 90%
- white < 100%
- black :outside

#####TOP-RIGHT#####
HR: Hertzsprung-Russell diagram
- x-axis log10(Teff) [K]
- y-axis log10(L/Lo)
- nb=log10(tfinal-tevol) (March 25th 2003)

#####BOTTOM-LEFT#####
YvsR: Abundances vs Radius

Radius: same as above

Abundances: (from surface to center)
- red = Hydrogen
- green = Helium
- red = Carbon
- green = Neon
- blue = Oxygen
- white/red = Silicium
- green = Nickel

Notes:
- darker tone means less abundant
- change of tone every 0.1 in Xi (sum_i(Xi)=1)
- next abundance color not shown until Xi<0.01 

#####BOTTOM-RIGHT#####
tcroc: central temperature (Tc) vs central density (rhoc)
- x-axis log10(rhoc) [g/cm^3]
- y-axis log10(Tc) [K]
